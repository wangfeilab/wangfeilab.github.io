## Principal investigator

### Fei Wang

**Assistant Professor, School of Artificial Intelligence, Anhui University**

Fei Wang works in bioinformatics and AI-driven drug discovery. His research interests include drug repositioning, drug–drug and drug–target interaction prediction, drug–disease association analysis, DTI/DTA modeling, and protein and variant-function prediction.
